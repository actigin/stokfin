Your phone is now a stock terminal

Stokfin is a mobile app for small businesses, warehouses and shops to count and track stock by scanning barcodes.

What you can do

  • Fast stock counts by scanning barcodes and QR codes with the camera
  • Manage products, categories and stock movements; see count differences
  • Export to Excel (XLSX) and print barcode labels
  • PC Barcode Bridge: use your phone as a wireless barcode scanner for a computer on the same Wi-Fi
  • Pro: automatic Google Sheets sync, team account and cloud backup

Your data stays with you

By default, data is kept only on your device. Creating an account and connecting Google Sheets are optional. See the Privacy Policy for details.
