Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Stokfin is a stock and barcode app developed by Pixel Fin. This policy explains what data the app processes and how you can control it.

1. Data that stays on your device

Your products, stock quantities, count sessions, stock movements and business profile are stored in the local database on your device. Unless you create an account, this data is not sent to Stokfin servers.

2. Stokfin account (optional)

If you create an account, your email address, name, business name and consent time are stored on Stokfin's servers at Supabase (Supabase Inc.). With Pro, a backup of your business data is also stored with your account. When you sign in with Google or Apple, only an authentication token, your name and your email address are received from that provider.

3. Camera

The camera is used only to read barcodes/QR codes, on the device. Images are not saved or shared.

4. Local network (PC Barcode Bridge)

When you turn this feature on, your phone sends the barcodes you scan to your computer on the same Wi-Fi network. The data does not go to the internet and can be protected with an optional access code.

5. Google Sheets sync (Pro, optional)

If you use "Connect with Google", Stokfin asks your Google account only for the drive.file permission. With it, Stokfin can only create and update the "Stokfin Stock List" spreadsheet it creates itself; it cannot see, read or change any other files in your Google Drive.

  • Your stock list (product name, barcode, category, quantity, price, etc.) is sent directly from your device to the spreadsheet in your Google account; it does not pass through or get stored on Stokfin servers.
  • The Google access token is used only on your device, for this sync.
  • You can remove the connection at any time in the app ("Disconnect") or on your Google account's Third-party connections page. The spreadsheet stays in your Drive; you can delete it if you wish.

Stokfin's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This data is not used for advertising, is not sold and is not used to train AI models.

6. Purchases

Subscriptions and lifetime licenses are processed by Google Play / the App Store and verified through RevenueCat (RevenueCat Inc.). An anonymous app ID (your account ID if you created an account) and purchase details are shared with RevenueCat; your card details are not.

7. Ads (free version)

The free version shows Google AdMob ads. AdMob may use device information such as the advertising ID. No ads are shown in Pro.

8. International transfers and retention

Supabase, RevenueCat and Google servers may be located outside your country. Account data is kept until your account is deleted; data on the device is deleted when you uninstall the app.

9. Account and data deletion

You can delete your account and all data linked to it (profile, business records, cloud backups) in two ways:

  • In the app: Settings → Account → "Delete account permanently". Deletion is immediate and permanent.
  • By email: If you can't access the app, send an email with the subject "Account deletion request" from your account's email address to pixelfinst@gmail.com. Your request will be completed within 30 days.

Local data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app. The spreadsheet in Google Sheets is in your Drive and under your control.

10. Children

Stokfin is an app for businesses and is not directed at children under 13.

11. Your rights and contact

For your rights to access, correct, delete and object under the GDPR and KVKK: pixelfinst@gmail.com.

12. Changes

If this policy is updated, the new version is published on this page and the "Last updated" date is changed.
